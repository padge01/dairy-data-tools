# nasem_feeds.csv

Feed composition (% of dry matter: NDF, starch, crude protein, fatty acids, water-soluble carbohydrates, ash; dry
matter as % as fed) for the 284 feeds of the feed library of the National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and
Medicine (2021), *Nutrient Requirements of Dairy Cattle*, 8th revised edition, taken from the library distributed with
the nasem_dairy package (Fieguth, Innes and Cant, University of Guelph).

These values are NASEM's, not this project's, and are not covered by this project's Apache-2.0 licence. NASEM's terms:
academic use is encouraged with appropriate acknowledgment; commercial use requires a copyright release from NASEM.
Dairy Data Tools uses them as book values, non-commercially, to show what a ration holds where the farm has no analyses
of its own, and says so on every card that uses them.
